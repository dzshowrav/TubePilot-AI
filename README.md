# TubePilot AI

An AI-powered YouTube Growth Studio for creators — **Discover → Research → Plan → Create →
Optimize → Publish → Analyze → Improve**.

> "Right topic, right audience, right packaging, right timing."
> TubePilot AI never claims to make videos go viral — it delivers growth opportunity detection,
> prediction, and optimization, always labeled as estimates, never guarantees.

## Roadmap (summary)

1. **Phase 1 — MVP:** auth, YouTube connect, dashboard, channel analyzer, trend radar, opportunity
   score, idea/script/title generators, basic analytics.
2. **Phase 2 — Growth Engine:** competitors, content gaps, Shorts Studio, Thumbnail Lab, SEO,
   calendar, comment intelligence, alerts, recommendations.
3. **Phase 3 — AI Agent:** orchestrator, Growth Agent, daily brief, background jobs, multi-provider
   AI, reports, subscriptions/credits.
4. **Phase 4 — Advanced:** thumbnail generation, A/B insights, multi-language, sponsorship
   assistant, agency mode, missions, continuous learning.
